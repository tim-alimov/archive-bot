# Product

## What it is

An open-source, self-hosted Telegram bot that turns scattered materials into an organized library. Teachers, creators, and small businesses sort their content into categories, and their audience browses it through simple buttons.

## Why it exists

In Central Asia, Telegram is where people are, so it's where knowledge gets shared. But a channel is a feed: older posts get buried, and finding one file means endless scrolling. This bot gives that content a shape. Instead of a stream, the audience gets a library.

## How it works

A visitor presses Start, picks a category, then picks a material. The bot sends it exactly as it was posted. Nothing to learn.

Admins manage everything inside the bot: create categories, then send or forward materials into them. Anything Telegram supports works, including documents, PDFs, APKs, videos, voice messages, stickers, and plain text.

Materials are stored in the owner's private Telegram channel, so content stays on Telegram and under the owner's control.

## Self-hosted

No sign-up, no payments, no central server. Each owner runs their own copy under their own bot name. Setup takes three values: the bot token, the admin IDs, and the storage channel ID.

## Principles

Simple to set up. Simple to use. Owned by whoever runs it.
