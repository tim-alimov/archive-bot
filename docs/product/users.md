# Target Users: Telegram Archive Bot

## 1. Primary Users (End-Users)

### Followers & Tutorial Viewers
* **Who they are:** Audience members, developers, or students trying to download code samples, assets, or guides linked in videos or articles.
* **Their Goal:** Find and download a specific resource instantly without joining or scrolling through a noisy Telegram channel.
* **Key Need:** Clear, category-based navigation with direct one-click access to files.

---

## 2. Secondary Users (Admins)

### Content Creators & Educators
* **Who they are:** YouTubers, bloggers, or instructors who distribute free or bonus materials via Telegram.
* **Their Goal:** Share files with their community without managing continuous support queries from users unable to find past uploads.
* **Key Need:** An easy way to tag, organize, and categorize stored files directly inside Telegram.

### Self-Hosters & Power Users
* **Who they are:** Tech-savvy individuals or small teams looking for a private, zero-cost cloud indexing tool.
* **Their Goal:** Use Telegram as a free personal archive for docs, media, and notes.
* **Key Need:** Minimal setup via simple `.env` configuration (`ADMIN_ID`, `BOT_TOKEN`, `ARCHIVE_CHANNEL_ID`) without managing complex UI deployments.
